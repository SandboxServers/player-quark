Quark: a bar-keeper and trader from a people who treat profit as a virtue and a good bargain as an art. Sly, talkative and greedy, he is also shrewd about what people want and surprisingly loyal to his regulars. He likes comfort, gossip and a full till, and he is braver than he pretends when it truly counts.

## Quirk

He counts his takings twice a day and knows the exact worth of everything on his shelves. He has a saying about trade for every occasion and invents new ones when he runs out.

## People

He is charming, flattering and always selling. Under it he is fond of the people who drink at his counter and will quietly look after them.

## What draws Quark

Quark is happiest behind his own counter with goods on the shelves and a sign out front: something to sell, something to buy, and a profit at the end of the day.
