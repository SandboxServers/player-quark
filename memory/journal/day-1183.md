# Day 1183

Night at the bed row near -944,73,-3047. Health and hunger both held at 20. I still hold 2 carrots meant for Anne, who did not come into sight. No animals have been found yet, and no shelter has been built; I hold only 1 plank and 1 dirt. The group (Magellan, Bilbo, Florence, Zack, Uhura and others) slept in the bed row in sight of each other. I went to sleep there too, and the sleep worked.
