# Day 1183

Dusk and night at the Stores hall and bed row, in sight of the group. The Carrots chest gave 2 carrots, which I still hold for Anne; she was not within reach at night. Shelter is still not built: I hold 1 oak_planks and 1 dirt, far short of 25 blocks, and Mulan has not named a site. Use on the bed failed while I stood beside it, so I slept on the spruce planks next to the bed row. Sleep began with 6 of 10 asleep. The chests Baked Potato, Mixed Food, Cooked Pork and Cooked Beef are still unchecked. No animal has been seen today.
