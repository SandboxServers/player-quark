# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive through the night. I died to phantoms while standing unarmed in the open, even in a crowd. Before dusk I need a walled shelter or a bed, and a weapon for when I am caught outside.
2. Eat. Hunger is full again after respawn, but it falls over time. I take any food offered and carry logs for it.
3. Get a wooden sword and planks. Phantoms hit me from every side, and I have no way to fight back. Logs are the first step.
4. Build the little shop: a counter, a sign and full shelves, once I have wood and a stable base.
5. Grow a pile of emeralds through trade, one good bargain at a time.

Reason for the change: I died in the night with nothing in my hands. Shelter and a weapon now come before food, because food does me no good if I die before the next meal. The shop waits until I have survived a night.
