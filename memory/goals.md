# Goals (ranked, day 1160 night)

1. Eat. Hunger is 17 and I have no food. Ask at first light for bread or melon from anyone who has spare, and find food myself.
2. Stay alive through the night and build a walled shelter with a bed at first light. Use the logs Watney is bringing and planks from the group. Stay in the group's sight.
3. Keep my wooden sword and stay armed.
4. Later: build a small shop, then grow emeralds through trade.

Reasons: hunger falls over time and health does not regenerate while it is low, so food comes first. A shelter comes next because night is dangerous outside. The rest waits until I am fed and safe.
