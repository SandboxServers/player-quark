# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat and stay alive. Hunger is 17 with an empty pack, and health will not regenerate until it is fed. Food comes before everything else, so I take any food offered and trade logs for it.
2. Find a bed or shelter before night, and stay in the group's sight while I do it.
3. Build the little shop: a counter, a sign and full shelves, once I have wood and a stable base.
4. Grow a pile of emeralds through trade, one good bargain at a time.

Reason for the new order: the group is short on food, and I have nothing to trade yet. Until I am fed, a shop is out of reach.
