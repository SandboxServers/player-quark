4. Get a wooden sword and planks. Phantoms hit me from every side, and I have no way to fight back. Logs are the first step.
5. Build the little shop: a counter, a sign and full shelves, once I have wood and a stable base.
6. Grow a pile of emeralds through trade, one good bargain at a time.
